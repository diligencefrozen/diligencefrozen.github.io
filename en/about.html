About PayKR

PayKR · Updated November 24, 2026

PayKR is an independent information service designed to help people explore businesses and online brands that may accept Apple Pay in South Korea.

Our purpose

The service organizes searchable place information so visitors can look up a business or address, browse categories and review location details before visiting. A separate online directory lists brands as a reference for checking payment options at checkout.

Service features

  • Map search: Search places and review displayed names and addresses through a map interface powered by Kakao Maps services.
  • Category browsing: Explore dining, shopping, convenience stores and cafés, hotels, fuel and charging, entertainment and other categories.
  • Online brand directory: Browse a reference list of online brands grouped by category.

Information limitations

PayKR does not process payments and does not operate Apple Pay terminals. Availability may differ by branch, terminal, payment method and merchant policy. Listings are provided for reference and do not guarantee that a payment will be accepted.

Independent service

PayKR is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Apple Inc., Kakao Corp., card issuers, payment networks or listed merchants. Apple Pay and third-party names and marks belong to their respective owners.
