Contact & Corrections

PayKR · Updated November 24, 2026

You can suggest corrections to place listings and brands or share ideas for improving PayKR.

Report a correction

When possible, include the following details:

  • Business or brand name
  • Store address or map link
  • What should be corrected and when you checked it
  • Publicly shareable supporting information

Open PayKR GitHub Issues

Privacy reminder

GitHub Issues may be public. Do not post card numbers, passwords, verification codes, private addresses, phone numbers or other sensitive personal information. For privacy-related requests, do not publish personal details in an issue; first check the public contact options on the operator's GitHub profile.

Response expectations

Suggestions may be reviewed and used to improve the service. We cannot guarantee an individual response or that every suggestion will be implemented.
