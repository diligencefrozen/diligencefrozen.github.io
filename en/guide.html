How to Use PayKR

PayKR · Updated November 24, 2026

PayKR helps you research possible Apple Pay locations before visiting a store or completing an online checkout.

1. Find a place on the map

  1. Open the PayKR map.
  2. Enter a business name, brand or address, or choose a category.
  3. Select a search result or map marker to review the displayed place name and address.
  4. Use the displayed information to identify the location you plan to visit.

2. Optional current-location feature

The current-location control may ask your browser for location permission. You can still use the service without sharing device location by searching manually. You can manage location permission in your browser settings.

3. Browse online brands

Open the online brand directory to search for a brand or filter by shopping, food, travel, convenience and other categories. The directory is a reference; confirm the payment methods shown on the merchant's checkout page.

4. Verify before payment

  • Look for an Apple Pay symbol near the entrance or payment terminal.
  • If the symbol is not visible, ask store staff whether Apple Pay is currently accepted.
  • Online acceptance does not mean that every physical branch accepts Apple Pay.
  • Actual payment can depend on the terminal, payment network, card issuer and merchant policy.
Important: PayKR search results are reference information. They do not guarantee merchant status or payment approval. Confirm acceptance with the merchant or checkout page.

5. Suggest a correction

Report a place or brand correction through GitHub Issues. Do not include card numbers, passwords, private addresses or other sensitive information in public issues.
