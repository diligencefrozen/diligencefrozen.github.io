What you can do with PayKR

Search for a place or brand, explore categories and use the displayed information as a starting point before making a payment.

Explore the map

Search by business name or address, or choose a category to browse places on the map.

Browse by category

Explore dining, convenience stores and cafés, shopping, hotels, fuel and charging, entertainment and more.

Check online brands

Browse a separate directory of online brands grouped by shopping, food, travel, convenience and other categories.

How to use the service

Search or choose a category

Open the map and enter a business name or address, or select a category.

Select a place

Choose a result or map marker to review the displayed business name and address.

Confirm before paying

Acceptance can vary by branch and terminal. Look for the Apple Pay symbol or ask staff before payment.

Important payment information

Directory listings do not guarantee a successful payment. Acceptance may vary by individual branch, payment terminal, checkout method, card issuer or merchant policy. Confirm availability at the store or checkout before relying on it.

Frequently asked questions

Is PayKR an official Apple service?

No. PayKR is an independent information service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Apple Inc.

Can I pay at every location shown?

Not necessarily. Listings are for research only. Check the specific branch and payment terminal before making a purchase.

Do I have to share my location?

No. You can search manually by business name or address. The browser location feature is optional.

How can I report incorrect information?

Use PayKR GitHub Issues. Do not post private or financial information in public issues.
