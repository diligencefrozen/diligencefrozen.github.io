Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This policy explains information that may be processed when you use PayKR and how third-party services are involved. PayKR currently operates as a static website and does not provide user registration, user accounts, payment processing or its own server-side user profile database.

1. Operator and scope

Project: PayKR · GitHub account: diligencefrozen
Scope: the PayKR information site, map service, online brand directory and related pages.

2. Information that may be processed

a. Website access information

Hosting and network providers, including GitHub Pages, may process technical access information such as IP address, requested page, access time, browser and device information to deliver and secure the website. Their own policies and systems govern this processing.

b. Map searches

When you search for a place name or address, your search query and requests needed for map functionality may be sent to Kakao Maps and its place-search services. See Kakao's privacy policy for details.

c. Location information

When you select the current-location control, your browser may request permission to use the device geolocation API. If permission is granted, coordinates are used in the browser to center the map. You can continue to search manually without granting location permission.

The currently deployed map code includes an approximate IP-based location fallback when GPS lookup fails. In that case, the browser sends a request to ipapi.co. That provider may process the request IP address and approximate regional information. If this fallback is removed in a future code update, this section must be revised to reflect the deployed behavior.

d. External chat

The map page may load Discord chat through WidgetBot Crate. If you use the widget or Discord, those providers may process IP address, device and browser information, and information you submit in chat under their own policies. See Discord's Privacy Policy and WidgetBot.

e. External fonts

Some pages may load Google Fonts. Google may process IP address and browser request information when fonts are requested. See Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising and cookies

PayKR may use Google AdSense to display ads. When AdSense code loads or ads are served, Google and third-party advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses and browser identifiers to deliver and measure ads and help prevent fraud or abuse.

Google and its partners may use information about visits to this or other websites to provide personalized advertising. You can manage personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also review choices for some third-party advertising cookies at aboutads.info.

More information: AdSense required content and How Google uses advertising technologies.

4. PayKR's own storage

PayKR does not currently operate user accounts or its own user database. Map search and location features work through the browser and external map services. GitHub, Kakao, Google, Discord and other providers may process information and cookies required for their own services.

5. Your choices

  • Allow or deny location permission in your browser settings.
  • Search manually by place name or address without using location permission.
  • Manage or block cookies in your browser; some third-party features may be limited.
  • Manage ad personalization through Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

6. Third-party links

PayKR may link to or use services provided by Kakao Maps, Discord, GitHub, Google and other third parties. Their own privacy policies and terms apply when you use those services.

7. Suggestions and privacy inquiries

Place corrections may be submitted through GitHub Issues. Issues may be public; do not include personal, financial or authentication information. For privacy-related requests, do not publish personal details in an issue. Check the public contact options on the operator's GitHub profile.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when service features, advertising settings or applicable requirements change. The effective date will be updated when changes are made.
